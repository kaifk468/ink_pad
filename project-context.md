## Benchmark: smooth Raw writing on a large page (2026-11-07, tag `smooth-writing-benchmark`)

Galaxy Tab S9 FE+ with S Pen, Chrome, 90 Hz, `?debug=on`, Zepto test diagram. Last-stroke line: gap between frames mid/95%/max ms, slow frames over 20 ms, draw ms.

| Case | Gap ms | Slow | Draw ms |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slimmed Zepto, 5,000 visible points, cached | 11/11/11 | 0% | 1.0-1.4 |
| Slimmed Zepto, 28,900 points, long strokes, cached | 11/11/17 | 0% | 1.0-2.5 |
| Unslimmed Zepto (~160k points), minimap closed | 11/11/11 | 0% | 0.8-1.3 |
| Unslimmed Zepto, minimap open, before the idle delay | 11/22/33 | 8% | 0.8-17 |
| Unslimmed Zepto, minimap open, final build | 11/11/11 | 0% | 0.8-1.5 |

What got there: cached path is the only default (direct limit 0), the whole page canvas is repainted each frame (`?dirty=box` restores the box repaint; the box repaint was the cause of late frames), strokes are thinned on pen-up, undo history shares strokes, the minimap is not redrawn while writing and waits 600 ms of quiet afterwards. Not measured yet: the redraw time after zoom or pan on a big page (felt under a second); the debug readout now has a timer for it (see Current Status). Not pushed at the time of tagging.

### 2026-11-17 — Fix: a refresh can no longer lose a diagram (local safety copy)
- Root cause (reproduced in headless Chrome, functional check only): when the chosen storage is locked (Google Drive sign-in not live after a reload or lapsed mid-session, or a device folder whose permission was not regranted) `readyRef` stays false and `scheduleAutosave` returned at once, so nothing was stored anywhere and there was no warning; a refresh lost everything drawn since. A new Drive diagram also had no local copy before its first upload, and with browser storage a reload inside the 1.2 s autosave delay lost the last strokes. Before the fix: Drive-locked desktop and tablet reloads lost the drawing every time, browser-storage reload at once lost it too.
- Fix: new `src/safetyCopy.ts`. Every change is written to a separate IndexedDB database `inkpad-recovery` (store `copies`), debounced 400 ms, only while no pen/touch stroke is active, in idle time, at most ~3 s late; flushed on pagehide/visibilitychange (plus a small localStorage emergency copy under 2M chars on pagehide), before opening/creating/importing another diagram and before switching storage. Works even while storage is locked. On load the copy is restored before the Drive sign-in wait or folder permission check; the backend copy is never applied over it; once storage is ready the restored sheet is saved and work left in other diagrams while locked is uploaded. `beforeunload` warns only while a change is in neither the safety copy nor the storage. The copy sits under every StorageBackend (browser, device folder, Drive, future backend). Saved .inkpad format unchanged.
- Checks: npm test 713/713, npm run build ok. Headless Chrome after the fix: all six reload cases (browser and Drive-locked, desktop and tablet, at once and after 3 s) keep the drawing; the leave-page warning appeared only on the immediate reloads. NOT verified on the Galaxy Tab or a real laptop: pen feel on a dense page with the idle writes, the warning on the tablet, reload with a locked device folder.

## Canvas and viewport

- White sheet: 2500 × 1500 canvas units, origin (0, 0).
- Drawable workspace: 2750 × 1650 units, bounds (-125, -75) to (2625, 1575), giving a 5% gray margin around the sheet.
- Stored strokes, rectangles, and connectors use canvas coordinates. View transforms affect display/input conversion only.
- Default zoom is 1×; limits are 0.1×–3×. Two-finger touch pinches to zoom; three-finger touch pans. S Pen remains the drawing/editing pointer.

## Drawing and editing

- Freehand strokes, hold-recognized rectangles/lines, UML connectors, selection/lasso, object movement/resizing, and snapshot undo/redo are implemented.
- Standard eraser stores partial erase paths and clears ink on the transparent ink layer, preserving the white sheet and gray background. Eraser widths are selectable.
- Delete Whole Shape removes the complete hit stroke, rectangle, or connector. S Pen contact/rubbing sweeps are sampled along the pointer path; deleting a rectangle detaches its connector endpoints.
- Main canvas, workspace minimap, and optional page overview use separate persistent transparent ink layers for correct erase compositing.

## Minimap

- Compact workspace overview is shown by default and maps the fixed drawable workspace with a viewport indicator.
- Expand reveals the separate page overview, which maps the full white sheet at a fixed scale.
- Minimap navigation changes the viewport, not drawing coordinates; navigator controls are isolated from drawing gestures.

## Lag over dense writing — 2026-11-01

- Symptom (tablet, deployed app): after roughly 40-50 words the ink lagged behind the pen; blank areas and fresh diagrams were smooth, and it came back as the page filled.
- Cause: every frame of a pen stroke redrew every item on the page into the ink layer, and also redrew the whole minimap. Cost grew with the amount of ink.
- Fix (`src/App.tsx`, `renderAll`): while a plain pen stroke is in progress, everything already on the page is painted once into `historyLayerRef` and reused each frame (valid only for the same items array and count, viewport, pixel density and canvas size; dropped as soon as no pen stroke is active; never used for erasers, benchmark runs or text editing). The minimap is refreshed at most every 300 ms while writing (`MINIMAP_LIVE_REFRESH_MS`).
- Measured in headless Chromium (pen events, mean ms per pointer move, empty page / 500 / 1500 / 3000 strokes): before 33 / 34 / 65 / 98 (p95 134); after 33 / 34 / 34 / 35 (p95 39). The final canvas pixels, mid-stroke and after the stroke, are identical to the old build.

- Still possible if lag remains: shrink the cost of the active stroke itself (it is redrawn whole each frame) and avoid redrawing the whole canvas for pan/zoom with lots of ink.

## Erase cleanup (2026-11-02)

Shape strokes (arrow/line) are never trimmed. Rectangles are treated as touched wherever they sit in the list, because connectors redraw their boxes.
